🎓 Edus School SaaS

A complete multi-tenant school management platform — admissions, academics, finance, store, examinations, approvals and a parent portal, all connected in one workflow.

Feature Mind Map — The 8 Modules
Edus School Portalone platform · every department

1 🚀 Setup & Branding

Get a new school running in minutes.

  • 6-Step Onboarding Wizard
  • School Branding & Logo
  • Smart Dashboard (KPIs)
  • General Settings

2 🎓 Admissions & Students

Bring students in, fast and accurately.

  • Admission & Enrolment
  • Bulk CSV Enrolment
  • Student Registry & Search
  • Mass Promotion
  • ID Cards & Letters

3 📚 Academic Structure

The backbone that everything else builds on.

  • Sessions & Terms
  • Classes & Year Groups
  • Departments
  • Subjects
  • Class Subjects (core/elective)
  • Grading Scale

4 🧑‍🏫 Staff & Roles

Assign people to the right responsibilities.

  • Staff Accounts
  • Form Teacher
  • HOD (Dept Head)
  • Year Group Master
  • Subject Teacher

5 📝 Exams & Approvals

Collect scores and govern them properly.

  • Result Entry (CA1–3, Theory, Practical)
  • Excel Template Import
  • Submit & Lock
  • 4-Stage Approval Workflow
  • HOD/Admin Unlock

6 📊 Reports & Analytics

Turn raw marks into insight.

  • Teacher Mark Sheet
  • Form Teacher Broadsheet
  • HOD Subject Master
  • Year Group Status Board
  • Admin Grade Distribution
  • Student/Parent Result Sheet

7 💰 Finance & Billing

Collect every naira owed, transparently.

  • Payments (Cash & Transfer)
  • Academic & Store Invoices
  • Fee Schedule & Structures
  • Scholarships & Discounts
  • Expenses & Profit/Loss
  • SMS Wallet & Subscriptions

8 🛒 Store, Parents & Web

Extend the school beyond the classroom.

  • Store Inventory & POS
  • School Calendar & Events
  • Public School Website
  • Parent Portal & Results
  • Parent Child Finance
