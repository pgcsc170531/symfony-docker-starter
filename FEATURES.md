# Edus School SaaS — Feature Catalogue (School Portal)

> A complete, multi-tenant school management platform. Every school gets its own
> private database and branded portal, covering admissions, academics, finance,
> store, results, approvals and a parent portal — all in one place.

---

## 1. Dashboard & Quick Setup

### 📊 Smart Dashboard
A single screen that tells you the health of your school at a glance — total
students, money invoiced vs. collected (outstanding debt), today's cash intake,
low-stock alerts, unpaid invoices and your SMS wallet balance. New schools are
greeted with a guided onboarding checklist.

### 🚀 6-Step Onboarding Wizard
Launch a new school in minutes. A step-by-step wizard takes you through identity
and logo, automatic class-structure generation (primary / secondary / tertiary
presets), the academic calendar, fee items and fee pricing — so you're ready to
operate on day one.

### 🎨 School Branding & Settings
Customise your school's identity: name, logo, hero image, colours and the public
website content that parents and visitors see on your own subdomain.

---

## 2. Admissions & Student Records

### 🎓 Student Registry
A searchable, filterable student directory with pagination, debtor/cleared
filters and split academic-vs-store debt badges. Drill into each student's
finance ledger and full profile (siblings, guardian, photo), edit details,
print ID cards, and export the whole directory to CSV.

### 📝 Admissions & Enrolment
A complete admission workflow: find or create the parent/guardian (with their
own login), register the child, auto-generate admission numbers and enrolment
records, apply discounts, and print a professional admission letter. Nigerian
state and LGA dropdowns are built in.

### ⚡ Bulk Enrolment
Onboard hundreds of students at once — either through a manual grid or by
uploading a 12-column CSV. The system matches class names automatically, creates
parent accounts and can auto-generate invoices for every new student.

### 📈 Mass Promotion
End-of-session promotion in a few clicks: select students, promote them to the
next class, mark repeaters, and auto-bill each student for the new session.

## 3. Academic Structure & Administration

### 🗓️ Sessions & Terms
Manage academic sessions and terms with single-active enforcement — starting a
new session or term automatically deactivates the previous one, so billing and
results always point at the right period.

### 📚 Classes (Smart Creator)
Create entire class levels in seconds: enter "JSS 1" and the number of arms and
the system generates `JSS 1A`, `JSS 1B`, `JSS 1C`… automatically.

### 📂 Year Groups
Group the arms of a level (e.g. all of JSS 1) under a single year group and
assign the classes into it — the foundation for form-teacher and year-group
workflows.

### 🏛️ Departments
Create academic departments (Sciences, Arts, Business, Islamic Studies…) to
organise subjects and define the span of control of each Head of Department.

### 📚 Subjects
Build your subject catalogue with codes and department assignment — the building
blocks of timetabling, teaching and results.

### 🗂️ Class Subjects
Decide which subjects each class offers per session, and tag them as **core** or
**elective** — so teachers only ever see the subjects that matter for each class.

### 🏅 Grading Scale
Define your own grading system (A1–F9, WAEC, NECO or custom) with score ranges,
points and remarks. Grades are applied consistently across entry, reports and
analytics.

### 👥 Staff Accounts
Create staff login accounts with role-based access (Principal, Bursar, Store
Keeper, Teacher) in seconds.

### 🧑‍🏫 Staff Assignments
Assign staff to academic roles with one click each: **Form Teacher**, **HOD
(Department Head)**, **Year Group Master** and **Subject Teacher** — with smart
search and duplicate protection.

## 4. Examinations, Results & Approvals

### 📝 Result Entry
A fast, friendly score-entry terminal for teachers. Enter CA1–CA3, theory and
practical marks per student, save drafts, submit for approval, download an Excel
template and bulk-import scores from it. Results lock after submission until an
authorised unlock.

### ✅ Multi-Stage Approval Workflow
A four-stage result sign-off that matches real school governance:
**Teacher → Form Teacher → HOD → Year Group Master**. Each level confirms,
approves or rejects; rejections automatically unlock the result for rework, and
HODs/Admins can unlock results for re-editing at any time.

### 📊 Reports & Analytics
A full reporting suite for every stakeholder:
- **Teacher mark sheets** — one class and subject with totals and grades.
- **Form Teacher broadsheets** — the whole class across all subjects, with
  averages and position.
- **HOD subject masters** — a subject across every arm, with class averages.
- **Year Group status boards** — approval progress for the entire year group.
- **Admin dashboard** — school-wide grade distribution (A1–F9) and averages.
- **Student/Parent result sheet** — individual results per term.

## 5. Finance & Billing

### 💳 Payments & Receipts
A complete payment engine: search a student, generate academic invoices, take
**cash** payments or generate **bank-transfer slips**, verify payment reference
codes, confirm with automatic SMS to the parent, and print receipts (including
walk-in store receipts).

### 🧾 Invoicing (Academic & Store Ledgers)
Two dedicated ledgers — **Academic Fees** and **Store Sales** — with status and
search filters, per-student invoice generation and detailed invoice views.

### 💰 Fee Schedule
A clear fee-schedule matrix showing what each class/level pays per term, with
recurring vs. one-time totals.

### 📋 Fee Items & Structures
Define chargeable line items (tuition, books, uniform) and assign them with
amounts to classes and terms — the pricing engine that drives every invoice.

### 🎖️ Scholarships & Discounts
Create discount rules (e.g. "Staff Child") and assign them to students with
duplicate protection. Discounts are applied automatically at billing time.

### 💸 Expenses
Record day-to-day expenses with categories and instant total-spent tracking.

### 📈 Profit & Loss
A date-range report that splits income into academic fees vs. store and subtracts
expenses to show your school's true net profit.

### 💳 SMS Wallet
Top up your school's messaging credit wallet, track balance and transactions,
and submit proof of payment for approval.



---

## 6. Store & Point of Sale

### 📦 Store Inventory
Manage products with stock levels and optional class-level targeting, with CSV
bulk import and duplicate prevention.

### 🛒 Point of Sale
A full POS terminal: search a student, build a cart with class-relevant items,
then check out either as a **student charge** (added to their store ledger) or a
**walk-in cash sale** (instant payment with automatic stock deduction).

---

## 7. Communication & School Website

### 📅 School Calendar & Events
Manage the school calendar and events, including Excel import, flash-notice
flags, and one-click **notify** to push announcements to parents.

### 🌐 Public School Website
Every school gets its own public website on its own subdomain — home page, about,
admissions and contact — automatically rendered from your branding and content,
so prospective parents always see a professional face.

---

## 8. Parent Portal

### 🏠 Parent Dashboard
Give parents their own login to see their children's invoices, payments and
urgent school notices at a glance.

### 📅 Parent Calendar
Parents can view the school calendar month by month.

### 💳 Child Finance
Parents can view each child's fee ledger and pay via bank-transfer slip, with
ownership verified so each parent only sees their own children.

---

*Everything runs in one secure, multi-tenant platform — each school isolated in
its own database, with role-based access for principals, bursars, teachers,
store keepers and parents.*
