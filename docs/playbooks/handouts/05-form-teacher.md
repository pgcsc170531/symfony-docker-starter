# Handout 05 — Form teacher (one class)

**Your job:** keep one class's register, behaviour notes and term remarks, then sign the
class's results off as a whole.
**Where you work:** your school's Edus address, menu group **Teaching → My Class**.

---

## Take the register

1. Open **Teaching → My Class**.
2. Tap **Attendance**, then today's date.
3. Everyone starts **Present**. Change only the pupils who are not:
   **L** late, **A** absent, **E** excused.
4. Tap **Save register**.

**What you should see:** *"66 present · 1 absent"* updates as you tap, and the marks are
still there when you come back.

If you made a mistake, change it and save again. The day is **updated**, never
duplicated.

## Rate behaviour

1. On **My Class**, tap **Behaviour**.
2. Pick a pupil, rate the traits on the 5-point scale.
3. Tap **Save & next student**.

Each trait saves as you tap it. A pupil with no rating shows `0/18`.

## Write the term remark

1. Tap **Remarks**.
2. Choose the pupil, type the remark (about two printed lines), tap **Save remark**.

The remark appears on the report card under your signature, and it locks when the term's
results are finally approved.

## Sign the class off

1. Open **Teaching → Result Approvals**.
2. Look at the **Form Teacher** section: every subject in your class, with its teacher
   and status.

**What you should see:** each subject reads **submitted** (waiting for you),
**not submitted**, **approved** or **rejected**.

3. When every subject is **submitted**, tap **Confirm Class**.

If something is missing the page refuses and names it: *"Cannot confirm yet — no submitted
results for: Mathematics, English Language…"*. Chase those teachers; do not look for a way
around it.

## Check the class results

1. **Teaching → Reports → Form Teacher Broadsheet.**

**What you should see:** every pupil against every subject, positions and averages.
Subjects nobody has marked show `—`, not `0`.

## What you should never do

- **Never confirm a class to "unblock" someone** — your confirmation is what tells the HOD
  the marks are complete.
- **Never mark a whole class present** without looking — an absent child's record matters
  in an emergency.
- **Never write a remark about a pupil's family** — the card is read by the pupil too.

## When it goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The register will not save | Check you are on the right date; a released term locks the register |
| A subject says *not submitted* for days | That teacher has not submitted — tell them, then the administrator |
| **Confirm Class** refuses | The message names the subjects; that is the to-do list |
| A pupil is missing from the class | The office changes class membership, not you |
