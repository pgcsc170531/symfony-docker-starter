# Handout 06 — HOD (department head)

**Your job:** read every submitted sheet from your department, then approve it or send it
back with a reason.
**Where you work:** your school's Edus address, menu group **Teaching**.

---

## See your department

1. Open **Teaching → Reports**, then **HOD Subject Master**.

**What you should see:** only the subjects in your department, with a class average per
subject. If it says *"No subjects in this department"*, the subjects are filed under
another department — ask an administrator to correct it under **Administration →
Subjects** (each row has a department selector).

## Read a sheet before you approve it

1. Open **Teaching → Result Approvals**.
2. In the **HOD** section, tap a **subject name** (or **View**).

**What you should see:** the submitted sheet in read-only form — every pupil, each
component, totals and grades, and the counts across the top:

| Pupils | Marked | Average | Credits | Passes | Fails |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

*Credits* is A1–C6, *passes* D7–E8, *fails* F9. There are no input boxes: you cannot
change a mark here, only the subject teacher can.

## Approve

1. Back on the approvals page, tap **Approve** next to the sheet.

**What you should see:** *"Subject results approved."* The sheet moves to the year group
master.

## Send it back

1. Type the reason in the box next to **Reject** — what the teacher must fix.
2. Tap **Reject**.

**What you should see:** *"Subject results rejected and unlocked for rework."* The teacher
sees your words on their sheet under **Sent back by the HOD**. A blank reason is refused,
because a sheet that comes back with no explanation comes back unchanged.

## What you should never do

- **Never approve a sheet you have not opened** — your approval is the department's
  statement that the marks are sound.
- **Never send a sheet back without a reason.**
- **Never edit a teacher's marks** — ask the administrator for an unlock if a correction
  is genuinely needed.

## When it goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Your department shows no subjects | Subjects are filed elsewhere — administrator, **Administration → Subjects** |
| A subject is missing from your queue | Its form teacher has not confirmed the class yet |
| "You do not review this class subject" | That subject belongs to another department |
| The teacher says they cannot resubmit | An admin must unlock the sheet, or the term is already released |
