# Handout 04 — Subject teacher (marks)

**Your job:** record the marks for the classes and subjects you are assigned, and hand
the sheet up for approval.
**Where you work:** your school's Edus address, menu group **Teaching**. This is the
screen most likely to be used standing in a classroom, so everything works on a phone.

---

## See what is yours

1. Open **Teaching → Result Entry**.

**What you should see:** one line per class and subject you teach, with the term. If the
page says you have no assignments, your administrator has not set them up yet — you
cannot fix that yourself, tell them.

## Enter marks

1. Tap the class and subject.
2. Type each pupil's CA scores and exam score in their row.
3. Tap **Save All Scores** whenever you like — drafts are private to you.

**What you should see:** the total and the grade fill in as you type. A pupil you have
not marked yet shows `—` in both columns, not `0` and not `F9`.

**Leave a mark blank if you do not have it.** A blank is honest; a zero is a result.

## Use a spreadsheet instead

1. On the sheet, tap **Download CSV Template**.
2. Open it — it already lists every pupil in the class, with any marks you have entered.
3. Fill the score columns. Do not add or remove rows.
4. Save as CSV, then tap **Upload CSV** and choose the file.

**What you should see:** the page reloads with the new totals. If a row cannot be read,
the report tells you which pupil and what was wrong — fix only those rows.

## Submit

1. Check the column is complete.
2. Tap **Submit for Approval**.

**What you should see:** the sheet becomes read-only and moves to the form teacher. You
cannot edit it again; an HOD or an admin can unlock it.

## If it comes back

Open the sheet. A red box at the top says **Sent back by the HOD** and quotes their
reason. Fix what they asked, then submit again.

## Check your report

1. Open **Teaching → Reports** and choose the class and subject.

**What you should see:** every pupil, the same totals as the entry sheet.

## What you should never do

- **Never type a grade** — the system calculates it from the scheme.
- **Never mark a pupil 0 because you have not finished** — leave it blank.
- **Never submit a half-filled column** to get it out of the way; it locks the sheet.
- **Never share your login** — your name is on the submission.

## When it goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The class list is empty | Your assignments are not set up — tell the administrator |
| "Results are locked" | Already submitted. Ask an HOD or admin to unlock |
| A pupil is missing from the CSV | The pupil is not in the class roster — check with the office, do not add rows |
| The total looks wrong | Check your scheme line at the top (number of CAs, exam mode) and tell the administrator if it is wrong |
