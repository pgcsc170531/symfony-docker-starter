# Handout 09 — Store keeper (books, uniforms, stationery)

**Your job:** keep the shelves counted and sell to pupils and walk-in customers.
**Where you work:** your school's Edus address, menu group **Store**.

---

## Add an item

1. Open **Store → Store Inventory**.
2. Fill **Item name**, **Category**, **Stock** and **Unit price**.
3. Leave **Class** empty if every class may buy it, or pick one class.
4. Submit.

**What you should see:** the item in the list with its stock. To change a price or a
count, use **Edit** on the row.

## Sell to a pupil (charged to their family)

1. Open **Store → Store POS**.
2. Search the pupil, tap them.
3. Choose the item and the quantity, tap **Add to Cart**.
4. Tap **Charge to Student**.

**What you should see:** a **store bill** with its own number. It does not change what the
family owes in school fees — the bursar and the parent both see them separately, on purpose.

## Sell to a walk-in customer (paid now)

1. Open **Store → Store POS**.
2. Tap **Walk-In** and type the buyer's name — it is printed on the receipt.
3. Choose the item and quantity, tap **Add to Cart**.
4. Tap **Complete Walk-In Sale**.

**What you should see:** a cash receipt with a reference number, ready to print. No pupil
is attached, so no child carries that debt.

## Check the shop

1. **Store → Store Sales** — every sale, pupils and walk-ins separated.
2. **Store → Store Inventory** — what is left.

**What you should see:** stock falling as you sell. If a sale fails with *"Only N left"*,
the shelf count is right and the request was too big.

## What you should never do

- **Never give stock out without a sale** — the count is the only record the school has.
- **Never put a fee item in the shop**, or a shop item on a fee bill. Fees are the
  bursar's, stock is yours.
- **Never write a walk-in sale to a pupil's name** to make the paperwork easier — the
  family would be billed for it.

## When it goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Stock issue for … only N remaining" | Correct the count with **Edit**, or sell fewer |
| The pupil is not found in POS | The pupil is not enrolled this term — use a walk-in sale or call the office |
| A store bill shows as unpaid | That is correct: the family pays it through the bursar or the parent portal, not at the shop |
| The receipt has no school name | **Settings → General** — an admin fixes that |
