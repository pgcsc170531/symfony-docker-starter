# Handout 07 — Year group master (final release)

**Your job:** release a whole year group's results to parents once every subject in it has
been approved — and not before.
**Where you work:** your school's Edus address, menu group **Teaching**.

---

## See where the year group stands

1. Open **Teaching → Reports → Year Group Status**.

**What you should see:** every class in your year group, each subject, its form teacher and
HOD stage, and the class average. A row reading *Form teacher approved · HOD pending* is
waiting on the HOD, not on you.

## Release the results

1. Open **Teaching → Result Approvals**.
2. In the **Year Group Master** section, read the sheet list.
3. Tap **Release {year group}**.

**What you should see:** *"Results for JSS 1 released — 5 subjects final-approved."*

If anything is missing, the page refuses and names it:

> Cannot release yet — these subjects still need HOD approval: Mathematics (JSS 1 A),
> English Language (JSS 1 A), Social Studies (JSS 1 A).

That list is your to-do list. Chase the HODs; there is no override.

**The release is all-or-nothing.** A class is never half-released, because a parent who
sees three subjects and not two cannot tell whether their child failed the rest.

## Check before you press it

Tap any **subject name** in the list. You get the sheet in read-only form with totals,
grades and credits — no editable fields. If a mark looks wrong, do not release; send it
back through the HOD.

## What parents get

The moment the year group is released:

- the parent's **My Results** page shows the report card, and
- the **Download PDF** button produces the A4 report sheet,
- and every family in the class receives one email about it (not one per subject).

The emails are **queued**, not sent while you wait — releasing a class of 67
returns in about a second. Delivery happens in the background and can take a few
minutes. If the page says some notifications could not be queued, tell the
administrator: the results are released, the mail is not.

Before release, parents see *"Results not released yet"* and the PDF is refused. Staff can
still preview the card.

## What you should never do

- **Never ask for the release to be bypassed** to satisfy one parent — release is what
  makes results official.
- **Never release in the middle of a term** unless the school has decided to close it.
- **Never correct a mark yourself** — that is the subject teacher's and HOD's job.

## When it goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "No class subjects in this year group" | The classes are not linked to the year group. Administrator: **Administration → Year Groups → Add a class** |
| Release refuses for days | The named subjects have no HOD. Administrator: assign one, or correct the subject's department |
| A parent says they cannot see the card | Check the release went through, then their child's class — release is per year group |
| The release takes a long time | It sends one email per family in the class. Press once and wait |
