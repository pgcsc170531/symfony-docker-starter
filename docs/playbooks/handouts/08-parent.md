# Handout 08 — Parent

**Your job:** see your child's bills, pay them, and read their results — without queueing
at the school.
**Where you work:** your school's Edus address, signed in with the phone number the school
has for you. On a phone, four tabs at the bottom are the whole menu.

---

## Sign in

1. Open the link the school sent you.
2. Enter the phone number or email the school has on record, and your password.

**What you should see:** **My Dashboard**, with a card for each of your children.

## See what is owed

1. Tap **Fees & Payments**.
2. Tap a child.

**What you should see:** every bill with its number, term, status and amount, and
**Download statement (PDF)** for a full record.

If a child has a scholarship, the bill shows the amount **after** the reduction with
`−₦6,200 scholarship` underneath, and the bill page names the scholarship. If you believe
a scholarship is missing, ask the school office — you cannot add one yourself.

## Pay

1. On the bill, tap **Pay Now** (online) or open the bill and use the school's bank details
   shown on the payment slip.
2. If you transferred at a bank or by phone, upload the proof of payment on the bill.

**What you should see:** the payment shows **Pending** — that is correct and your balance
does not change yet.

3. When the school confirms the money in their bank, the payment becomes **Paid** and you
   can download the receipt.

**Never transfer to an account that is not printed on the school's slip.** If the slip says
the school has not set its bank details, call the school.

## See results

1. Tap **My Results**.

**What you should see:** the report card for the term — subjects, marks, grade, position
and attendance — and **Download PDF** for a printable copy.

If it says **"Results not released yet"**, the school has not finished approving that
term's marks. Nothing is missing from your account.

## Apply for a place

1. Tap **Admission Applications**, then **Apply**.
2. Fill in your details and your child's details, and submit.

**What you should see:** the application on your dashboard with a status. Keep the
reference — that is what the school asks for.

## What you should never do

- **Never share your login** — it shows your children's bills and results.
- **Never pay into a personal account**, even if the message looks official. Only the
  account on the school's own slip.
- **Never upload the same proof twice** if the first says Pending — ask the office first.

## When it goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Child missing from your dashboard | The school links children to your phone number — ask the office |
| The bill is more than you expected | Open the bill: fees, then any scholarship, then the total |
| "We could not verify that reference" | Re-check the number on the slip, or search your child again |
| Pending for more than a few days | Call the school with the reference number; they must see the money in their bank |
| Cannot open another child's bill | Each family sees only its own children — that is deliberate |
